How to Read BuoyFM

        
Visual Key & Sound Guide

      

      
    


    
    

      

        BuoyFM turns live ocean conditions from floating sea buoys into a 5-layer visual stack and a relaxing soundscape.
      


      
      

        

          1. Bubble Size = Wave Height
        


        
        

          

            

              
            

            
Calm

            
Under 1m

          


          

            

              
            

            
Moderate

            
2m – 3m

          


          

            

              
            

            
Big Swell

            
4m – 6m+

          

        


        

          Taller waves make each bubble expand. In rough seas, neighboring bubbles grow large enough to touch and overlap.
        

      


      
      

        
2. Ring Shapes & Speed


        

          
          

            
            

              Hollow Target Rings: Show quick, wind-blown surface chop on the top layers.
            

          


          

            
            

              Layered Solid Bubbles: Show deep ocean swell rolling beneath the surface.
            

          


          

            
            

              Pulse Speed = Time Between Waves: Short choppy waves (3–5 sec apart) ripple fast; long ocean swells (12–14 sec apart) breathe slowly.
            

          

        

      


      
      

        
3. Color = Water Temperature

        

          

            
Cold

            
Navy • ~6°C

          

          

            
Mild

            
Teal • ~18°C

          

          

            
Warm

            
Burgundy • 28°C+

          

        

        

          Both the bubble colors and the dark background shift automatically from icy navy in Alaska to warm burgundy in the Caribbean.
        

      


      
      

        
4. The 5 Layers & Sounds

        

          Tap any layer button at the bottom to solo or mix what you see and hear (top to bottom):
        

        

          

            1. Wind / Gust
            Wind ripples • Soft breeze
          

          

            2. Surface Chop
            Hollow rings • Gentle chime
          

          

            3. Swell Crest
            Wave size • Rolling surf
          

          

            4. Wave Period
            Wave tempo • Deep tide pulse
          

          

            5. Thermal Base
            Water temp • Warm low hum
          

        

      


      
      

        

          Public Ocean Data
          Every 10–60 Min
        

        

          All buoy information is free and publicly available from the NOAA National Data Buoy Center. Sensors bolted to floating ocean buoys measure wind, waves, and water temperature and broadcast fresh readings via satellite every 10 to 60 minutes.
