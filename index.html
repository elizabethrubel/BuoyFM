How live NOAA buoy readings drive the 5 isometric planes

PLANE 50 // WIND & GUST (WSPD, GST, WDIR) Air Layer

Audio Stem: Bandpass-filtered white/pink wind hiss; gusts (GST) sweep the filter resonance higher.

PLANE 75 // SURFACE CHOP (APD) Ripple Layer

Visual: Average Wave Period (APD) controls the pulsing speed of the center pinhole dots and target rings.
Audio Stem: Rhythmic shortwave hydrophone telemetry pings clocked to the APD chop frequency.

PLANE 100 // SWELL CREST (WVHT, MWD) Primary Wave

Visual: Significant Wave Height (WVHT) dilates the outer ring radius along Mean Wave Direction (MWD).
Audio Stem: Deep brown-noise ocean surf wash whose cresting volume scales with wave height in meters.

PLANE 125 // WAVE PERIOD (DPD) Deep Harmonic

Visual: Dominant Wave Period (DPD, 3s–14s) sets the wavelength and breathing tempo across all planes.
Audio Stem: Sub-bass harmonic Fifth oscillator pulsing slowly at the exact swell period (1 / DPD Hz).

PLANE 150 // THERMAL BASE (WTMP, PRES) Heat Mass

Visual: Sea Surface Temp (WTMP) interpolates the 5-color concentric palette from Polar Cobalt (8°C) to Equatorial Vermilion (30°C) and expands inner thermal cores.
Audio Stem: Warm foundational sine drone whose pitch and harmonic warmth rise with water temperature.
